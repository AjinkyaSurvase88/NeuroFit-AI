How to Use Neuro Fit AI

📱 Mobile Setup

  • Prop your phone against a wall or use a tripod.
  • Ensure your full body (or upper body) is visible in the frame.
  • Make sure the room is well-lit for accurate AI detection.
  • Keep the phone about 6-8 feet away.

💪 Pushups

  • Position the camera to your side profile.
  • The AI tracks your shoulder, elbow, and wrist.
  • Go down until your elbow bends to at least 90°.
  • Come all the way up to lock out your arms.

🏋️ Pullups

  • Position the camera to see your entire upper body and the bar.
  • Start from a dead hang.
  • Pull up until your chin is above the level of your shoulders/hands.
  • Return to a full dead hang to complete the rep.
